## Do not cross-walk ROAS blindly
If finance needs incrementality, use holdouts or MMM, and still do not invent lift percentages. Linear and CTV count exposure differently.
A last-click digital ROAS next to a TV halo number is a slide, not a study. Label the clock.

## Creative for a bigger screen
CTV still needs a first-second interrupt, just on a bigger screen and often with sound on. Safe zones and length differ from Reels.
Do not paste a 9:16 UGC hook onto CTV without a layout pass. That is a test, not a law.

## Buying TV is not eonik
eonik does not traffic linear or CTV. Production of an on-brand file can still live on Mac.
You approve the cut. You still buy dayparts or digital TV inventory elsewhere.

## CTV vs linear TV
|  | Linear TV | CTV |
| Buy | Dayparts and footprints | Digital streaming inventory |

### Lean linear when
* Footprint and daypart are the job finance understands
* You have an incrementality design, not a vanity ROAS
* Creative can live as a longer master

### Lean CTV when
* You want digital buying on a TV screen
* You can still fund an incrementality read
* You will adapt the opening for a bigger screen, not paste Reels
